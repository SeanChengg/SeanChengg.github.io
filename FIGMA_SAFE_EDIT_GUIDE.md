# Safe Way to Edit Text & Images in Figma (Without Ungrouping)

## ✅ Safe Method: Use Layers Panel to Find Elements

You don't need to ungroup! You can find and edit elements directly through the Layers panel.

---

## Step 1: Open Layers Panel

1. **Look at the left sidebar** in Figma
2. **Find the "Layers" panel** (it shows all your frames and groups)
3. If it's collapsed, click the **arrow icon** to expand it

---

## Step 2: Find Text Elements

### To find text:
1. **In the Layers panel**, look for layers with **"T" icon** (text layers)
2. **Common text layer names:**
   - `"We make the invisible visible"`
   - `"Data & Measurement"`
   - `"Tracking and attribution..."`
   - `"Discover more"`
   - `"Our services"`

### To edit text:
1. **Click directly on the text layer** in the Layers panel
2. **Double-click the text** on the canvas (or press `T` key)
3. **Edit the text** directly
4. **Press `Esc`** when done

**No ungrouping needed!** Text layers are always editable.

---

## Step 3: Find Image Elements

### To find images:
1. **In the Layers panel**, look for:
   - Layers with **image icon** (small picture icon)
   - Frames named like: `"Screenshot...avif"`, `"image.png"`, `"683c37998875d..."` (hash names)
   - Frames with type `RECTANGLE` that have image fills

### To replace images (SAFE method):
1. **Click on the image layer** in Layers panel (or on canvas)
2. **Look at the right panel** → **Fill section**
3. **You'll see a thumbnail** of the current image
4. **Click the thumbnail** → Choose **"Replace image"**
5. **Select your new image** from your computer

**This replaces the image without ungrouping!**

---

## Step 4: Use Search in Layers Panel

### Quick search:
1. **Click in the Layers panel**
2. **Press `Cmd + F`** (Mac) or `Ctrl + F` (Windows)
3. **Type part of the text** you're looking for (e.g., "invisible")
4. **Figma will highlight** matching layers
5. **Click the highlighted layer** to select it

---

## Step 5: Navigate Nested Groups (Without Ungrouping)

### To see inside groups:
1. **In Layers panel**, look for **arrow icons** (▶ or ▼)
2. **Click the arrow** to expand/collapse groups
3. **Navigate deeper** by expanding nested groups
4. **Click directly on the layer** you want to edit

**You're just viewing the structure, not ungrouping!**

## Pro Tips for Safe Editing

### ✅ DO:
- Use **Layers panel** to navigate
- **Click directly** on layers in Layers panel
- **Double-click text** to edit (or press `T`)
- **Use Fill panel** to replace images
- **Lock layers** you don't want to move (`Cmd + L`)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Don't ungroup everything at once
- Don't delete groups without checking what's inside
- Don't flatten everything (makes it harder to edit later)

---

## If You Can't Find Something

### Try this:
1. **Click on the element** on the canvas (the visual element you see)
2. **Look at the Layers panel** - it will highlight the selected layer
3. **Check the right panel** - it shows the layer name and properties
4. **Note the layer name** - use it to find it again later
