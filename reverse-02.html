Configuration of Actuating Wires
LIMS2 is a robotic arm that achieves human-safe, high-speed motion by minimizing joint inertia while maintaining high structural stiffness. The actuators and reduction gears are placed at the proximal end of the arm, and motion is transmitted to the joints using a cable-driven system combined with block-and-tackle pulleys. This configuration amplifies output force while keeping the distal sections lightweight, closely matching the mass and dynamic properties of a human arm.
