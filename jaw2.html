Creature Animatronic

02
Inspired by a prior jewelry design. The creature's cranium and mandible blend cosmetic exteriors with internal mechanics, modeled in ZBrush and Fusion 360. Modular skeletal structure combines 3D-printed and cast bronze parts, including a CNC-carved maple headpiece. A pneumatic cylinder drives the mandible's motion, demonstrated in a temporary setup using air and DC power.
Casted Bronze, Steel, Hardwood, Bearings, Bimba Double Acting Pneumatic Cylinder and Solenoid Valve, 24V Supply
