This wearable accessory features a pneumatic cylinder that opens and closes the bronze jaw through controlled piston extension and retraction. Users operate the jaw by placing their hand inside the mechanical body and pressing an internal button. The device connects directly to compact air and power supplies.

Referencing a prior jewelry design, the creature's cranium and mandible blend cosmetic exteriors with internal mechanics, modeled in ZBrush and Fusion 360. Modular skeletal structure combines 3D-printed and cast bronze parts, including a CNC-carved maple headpiece. A pneumatic cylinder drives the mandible's motion, demonstrated in a temporary setup using air and DC power.
Casted Bronze, Steel, Hardwood, Bearings, Bimba Double Acting Pneumatic Cylinder and Solenoid Valve, 24V Supply
