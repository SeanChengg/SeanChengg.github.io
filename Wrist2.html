This diagram illustrates the design of three-DOF spherical rolling joint.

 

Three parallel links connect the base to the distal frame, allowing smooth rolling motion.

 

A pair of tension wires is pre-wound around the blue pulley four times to amplify the pulling force.

 

The green-marked shaft, equipped with two universal joints, transmits rotational motion from the bottom servo to the distal end.
